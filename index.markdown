## Why we exist
Fewer than 20% of computer science students are women, which leaves women and non-binary people mostly *using* technology rather than *building* it. We organize **free coding workshops for women, non-binary and trans people** without prior experience, led by skilled volunteer coaches who want to grow the number of women\* in tech. We keep groups small — **one dedicated coach for every two or three learners** — in a cozy space where **questions are always welcome** and there is no pressure or competition.

## The people behind code curious
code curious is run entirely by volunteers who believe a more diverse tech industry is a better one. The overwhelmingly positive feedback from our attendees and coaches is what keeps us going.
